# Teaching

My teaching at Leibniz University Hannover connects the foundations of quantum information with current research in quantum computing and information theory.

{% include courses.html %}

For schedules, registration, and course materials, visit [Stud.IP](https://studip.uni-hannover.de/index.php?again=yes) and search for the course title. The course pages above provide further details.

## Further reading

These freely available resources offer different routes into the subject:

- [Quantum Computing: Lecture Notes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.09415), **Ronald de Wolf** — quantum computing from a computer science perspective, with exercises and mathematical background.
- [From Classical to Quantum Shannon Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.1445), **Mark M. Wilde** — a detailed route from quantum information fundamentals to communication and information theory.
